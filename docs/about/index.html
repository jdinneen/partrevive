About PartRevive

Short answer: PartRevive is a new one-person shop, launched October 2026, that 3D-prints replacement plastic parts to order.

Why this exists

A small plastic part breaks, and the maker sells only the whole assembly, or no longer sells it at all. We make the single part you need, in a material chosen for how it is used.

How we work

  1. You send a photo and the model number.
  2. We quote within 24 hours on business days, including whether printing is a sensible fix.
  3. You approve, we design and print it, and we ship it. Lead time is 5 to 10 business days after approval.

We do not stock parts. Everything is made to order, up to about 250 mm.

Materials

  • PETG for general indoor parts
  • ASA for sun and heat
  • PA-CF (carbon-fibre nylon) for high loads
  • TPU for flexible parts

What we will not make

Safety-critical parts such as brake or steering components, battery contacts and other live electrical parts, children's items, and surfaces that touch food. We also do not claim our parts are equivalent to, or certified against, the original maker's part.
