Why it breaks

The V11 trigger is a small plastic lever that you press every time you clean, and it snaps at its pivot. The vacuum works, but you cannot turn it on without the trigger.

OEM situation

The original trigger is sold only as part of a whole handle. The most-downloaded free fix has 2,630 downloads, which shows how common the failure is.

What we make

We print only the plastic trigger, in PA-CF, carbon-fibre nylon, because the pivot takes repeated load. We do not touch electrical parts: the switch, battery, motor and wiring are untouched, and you fit the trigger yourself. We need photos of the broken trigger and the handle slot, with a coin for scale.

Frequently asked questions

Do you repair the switch or battery?

No. We do not touch electrical parts. We print only the plastic trigger, and the switch, battery and wiring are not part of the service.

Is a printed part as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed PA-CF is strong in most directions, but a layer-line direction exists: the part is weakest where layers pull apart. We orient the print so the main load runs along the layers, and we tell you if a part is a poor candidate. The pivot is the part that fails, so we orient the print so the layers do not split there.

How do I measure or photograph the part?

Photograph the broken trigger, the handle slot and the pivot pin, with a coin for scale on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it, from the top, the side and the mounting end. Also send the model number, usually on a sticker inside the door, under the unit or on the product label. If you still have a broken piece, photograph that too.

Will fitting it affect my warranty?

It may. Check your Dyson warranty before you open the handle, since opening the vacuum is your decision.

Why not buy the whole handle?

The whole handle is the only original option and costs far more than a trigger. A printed trigger replaces just the broken plastic.
