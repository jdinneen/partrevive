Why it breaks

Plastic fridge handles and latch catches snap from sun exposure and from vibration on the road. The parts are specific to each fridge model, so a handle that looks close often does not fit. A broken latch can leave the door swinging open while you travel.

OEM situation

Original parts range widely. A Dometic DMR702 handle lists at about $20.99, a DM2672 or DM2872 handle set of two at $64.99, an NRX130C handle assembly at $42.02 on sale against $66.81, and a Norcold latch at $2.95. We price from $24 and quote by model.

What we make

We print in ASA, a plastic made to resist sun and heat. We print only the plastic handle or latch catch. We do not make or touch anything to do with gas, burners or electrics. We need the fridge model number, usually on the label inside the fridge, and photos of the broken part and its mounting points with a coin or ruler beside them.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my Dometic or Norcold fridge?

We match the part from your model number and photos. The models we list are a starting point, and we confirm the fit with you before printing.

Is a printed handle as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed layers are weakest where they pull apart, so we orient the print so the main load runs along them. We tell you if a part is a poor candidate.

How do I measure or photograph the part?

Photograph the handle or latch from the front, side and back on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it, plus the door where it mounts. Send the fridge model number, usually on a label inside the fridge, and note the screw hole spacing if you can measure it.

Do you work on gas or electrical parts?

No. We print only the plastic door handle or latch catch, never anything connected to burners, gas or electrics.

Can you make a matching pair of handles?

Yes, for models that use two. Tell us the quantity and we quote a lower price per handle.
