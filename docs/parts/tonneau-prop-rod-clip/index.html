Why it breaks

The small plastic clip that holds the prop rod or strut cracks or is lost, so the cover will not stay propped open. That typically happens after sun, cold and many open-close cycles. The cover itself is usually fine.

OEM situation

The cover maker typically sells these clips only as part of a hardware kit, though we have not verified this for every model. Free printable files exist for some covers, but they need to match your exact cover and a photo with scale. We make a single clip to your photo instead.

What we make

We print ASA because a clip on a truck bed sits in sun and weather all year, and ASA is the UV and heat resistant choice. We do not use PLA, which softens in heat. We need a photo of the old clip or the empty mounting spot, with a coin or ruler in the shot, plus the cover brand and model and your truck year. We make trim and hardware clips only, not safety-related parts.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my tonneau cover?

We target BAKFlip and UnderCover covers and confirm the fit from your photos, cover model and truck year before printing.

Is a printed clip as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed layers are weakest where they pull apart, so we orient the print so the load runs along the layers. A prop rod clip holds a light load, but tell us if your cover is heavy.

Will it handle heat and sun on a truck bed?

That is why we use ASA, which is UV and heat resistant. PLA softens in heat, so we do not use it.

How do I photograph the clip for a fit check?

Photograph the clip from the front, side and back, and the spot on the cover or rod where it fits, on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it. Send your cover brand, model and truck year.

Are you affiliated with the cover makers?

No. Brand names tell you what the part fits and nothing more. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any cover brand, and we do not make safety-related car parts.
