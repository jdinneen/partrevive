Why it breaks

The plastic stem or socket of a door holder cracks or loses its grip, and the door then swings in the wind. Holders also go missing when the screws strip out. Some campers use a shape the common store-bought catch does not fit.

OEM situation

A Camco 3 in door catch lists at $8.59, and an RV Designer E257 angled holder ranges from about $7.99 to $31 on eBay. Our value is in odd shapes the generic part does not fit. We price from $12.

What we make

We print in ASA, a plastic made to resist sun and heat. We need photos of the broken holder and of the spot on the door and frame where it mounts, with a coin or ruler beside them. We match the screw spacing and shape from your photos.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my door?

If your holder is a plunger-and-socket style, we match it from photos. We confirm the fit with you before printing.

Is a printed holder as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed ASA is strong in most directions but weakest where the layers pull apart, so we orient the print to put the load along the layers. We tell you if a part is a poor candidate.

How do I measure or photograph it?

Photograph the holder from the front, side and back on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it. Photograph the mounting spots on the door and frame too, and measure the screw hole spacing if you can. Send the camper make and model if you know them.

Can you copy an angled or 90 degree holder?

Often, yes. Send clear photos from several sides and we tell you whether we can match the shape.

Can I order a few at once?

Yes. Tell us the quantity and we quote a lower price per holder.
