Why it breaks

The clips that hold folding tines on a dishwasher rack get brittle with heat and detergent, and snap or wear out. Once one is lost, the tine will not stay upright and plates tip over.

OEM situation

An original clip kit, number 00428344, costs about $50. Our set of four starts at $16, and the final figure depends on the clip shape.

What we make

We print in ASA because dishwasher heat can warp ordinary hobby plastics. We make no food-contact claims; the clips sit on the rack and are not meant to touch food. Heat and water age any plastic, so treat the clips as a repair that may need renewing. We need photos of a surviving clip, the tine and the rack, with a coin for scale.

Frequently asked questions

Can a printed clip survive dishwasher heat?

ASA tolerates heat better than ordinary hobby plastics, but we cannot promise the lifespan of the original. We make no certification claims.

Is a printed part as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed ASA is strong in most directions, but a layer-line direction exists: the part is weakest where layers pull apart. We orient the print so the main load runs along the layers, and we tell you if a part is a poor candidate. Tine clips carry a light load, so the main risk is heat ageing, not strength.

How do I measure or photograph the part?

Photograph the clip from the side and the end, and the tine where it fits, with a coin in the shot on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it, from the top, the side and the mounting end. Also send the model number, usually on a sticker inside the door, under the unit or on the product label. If you still have a broken piece, photograph that too.

Are the clips food safe?

We make no food-contact claims. The clips are on the rack, away from where food sits, but we do not certify them.

Which Bosch models fit?

We match the clip from photos rather than a model list. Send the rack and the clip and we confirm the fit.
