Why it breaks

The plastic paddle or lever on a screen door latch cracks with sun exposure and daily use, and the spring-return parts wear out. Owners report the paddle is hard to find on its own. A small broken lever leaves the whole door unable to latch.

OEM situation

Replacements are typically sold only as a full latch kit. One zinc kit lists on Amazon through a third-party seller at $17.09, and one forum poster said their dealer had discontinued the replacement. We price from $22 for just the part you need.

What we make

We print in ASA, a plastic made to resist sun and heat, which suits a part that lives on the outside of the camper. We need a photo of the latch from the front, side and back with a coin or ruler beside it, and we need to know whether it is a left-hand or right-hand door. Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my camper screen door?

If your latch looks like a paddle or lever style used on Forest River and similar doors, we match it from photos. We confirm the fit with you before printing.

Is a printed latch as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed ASA is strong in most directions but weakest where the layers pull apart, so we orient the print to put the main load along the layers. We tell you if a part looks like a poor candidate.

How do I measure or photograph the latch?

Photograph the latch from the front, side and back on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it. Tell us if the door is left hand or right hand, and measure the distance between the screw holes if you can. Send the model number if there is one.

Do you make the spring too?

We print the plastic paddle and lever parts. Springs are metal, so we typically suggest reusing yours or buying a small spring locally, and we will say so in the quote.

Can I order a left and right pair?

Yes. Tell us how many of each hand you need and we quote a lower price per latch.
