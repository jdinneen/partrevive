Why it breaks

The hinge cracks and splits at the pin, and reviewers report Igloo hinges splitting from stress after a few years. The cooler body is usually fine, but a lid without a hinge will not stay on.

OEM situation

A universal Igloo hinge set is about $10 to $12 a pair, and a Coleman set of four hinges is about $10.43. A hinge for the 150 to 165 qt Igloos is $35.99. Many free models also exist, so we compete on matching your exact hinge from photos, from $14.

What we make

We print in PETG, a tough everyday plastic, with layers oriented so the pin load runs along them. The hinge screw spacing and pin diameter decide the fit, so we need a photo of the cracked hinge beside a coin or ruler and one of the lid and body mounting points. Large coolers take bigger hinges, up to our 250 mm size limit.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my Igloo or Coleman cooler?

We match it from photos and the hinge screw spacing, and confirm the fit with you before printing. Tell us the brand, quart size and any model number from the cooler.

Is a printed hinge as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed PETG is strong, but it is weakest where layers pull apart, so we orient the print along the pin load. We tell you if your hinge is a poor candidate.

Will it last outdoors in the sun?

PETG is fine for ordinary outdoor use, but we do not give a UV rating. If the cooler sits in strong sun all day, tell us when you order and we can discuss ASA.

Can I get a set for both sides?

Yes. Tell us how many hinges you need and we quote a lower price per hinge.

How do I measure or photograph it?

Measure the distance between the hinge screw holes and the pin diameter, or photograph both beside a ruler. Add photos of the hinge from the top, side and mounting face on a plain background.
