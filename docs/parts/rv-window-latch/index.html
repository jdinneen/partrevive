Why it breaks

RV window latches live in direct sun and heat. Over the years the plastic turns brittle, and a latch that has worked for a decade snaps the day you open the window. Many are sold only as part of a larger window assembly, so a small break can look like a costly repair.

OEM situation

Typically the original latch is hard to buy on its own and is often sold as part of an assembly. Other printed-latch sellers charge roughly $7 to $24 per latch, which is the range we price within.

What we make

We print in ASA because it resists UV and heat far better than ordinary hobby plastics, which matters on a sun-facing window. We match the shape from your photo and, if needed, from the broken pieces. We need a clear photo with a coin or ruler, and the window brand if you know it.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my RV window?

If your latch looks like the Elixir, Hehr or Kinro style, probably. We do not stock parts, so we confirm the match from your photos before quoting and tell you if we are unsure.

Is a printed part as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed ASA is strong in most directions, but a layer-line direction exists: the part is weakest where layers pull apart. We orient the print so the main load runs along the layers, and we tell you if a part is a poor candidate. A latch holds a window closed against wind, not a person's weight.

How do I measure or photograph the part?

Photograph the latch from above and from the side, plus the window frame where it mounts on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it, from the top, the side and the mounting end. Also send the model number, usually on a sticker inside the door, under the unit or on the product label. If you still have a broken piece, photograph that too.

Why ASA instead of cheaper plastic?

ASA is made for outdoor sun and heat. Cheaper plastics such as PLA soften in a hot vehicle, so we do not use them for RV parts.

Can I order a pair or a set for the whole RV?

Yes. Tell us how many windows you have and we quote a lower price per latch for multiples.
