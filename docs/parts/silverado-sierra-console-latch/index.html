Why it breaks

The console latch breaks and the lid will not stay shut, so it flaps open over bumps and corners. It is a small part, but the console is used every drive. People often cannot tell whether the latch or the lid itself has failed.

OEM situation

A cheap fix exists: one retailer lists an aftermarket 2-pack of latches (GM 20864151/53/54) for $8.99, and a single latch for $25.95. One reviewer there said a dealer wanted over $200 and would not sell just the latch (an anecdote, not a price we can confirm). If the cheap latch fits your truck, buy it. A made-to-fit printed part makes sense when your year or colour does not match, the part is discontinued, or the latch keeps breaking and you want a stronger material.

What we make

We print PETG, which holds up in a warm cabin where PLA softens, and we can use ASA if the vehicle sits in strong sun. We do not use PLA for car interiors. Frequently asked questions

Why not just buy the $8.99 aftermarket pair?

If it fits your truck and you are happy with it, do that. Ours is for when the year or colour does not match, the part is discontinued, or the latch keeps breaking and you want a stronger material.

Is a printed latch as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed layers are weakest where they pull apart, so we orient the print so the main load runs along the layers. We tell you if the part is a poor candidate.

Will it handle heat in a parked truck?

PLA softens in a hot car, so we do not use it. We print PETG for cabins and can use ASA if the vehicle sits in strong sun.

How do I photograph the latch for a fit check?

Photograph the latch and its mating spot on the lid from the front, side and underside, on a plain background with a coin or ruler. Send your year, model and any part number on the old piece. The photo also tells us whether the latch or the lid is the part that failed.

Do you make safety-related car parts?

No. We make interior and trim parts only, and we do not make airbag, seatbelt, brake, steering, lighting or fuel parts.
