Why it breaks

A door bin that holds a gallon of milk takes a lot of weight at its corners and lower edge. Cold, aging plastic cracks there, and the bin drops or sags. Replacing the whole door is not an option, so the bin is the part people hunt for.

OEM situation

Original Samsung bins typically cost $115 to $140 and are often out of stock. We price from $55 for a typical bin, and the final figure depends on size.

What we make

We print in PETG, which stays tough in the cold. We rebuild the bin to your photos and measurements. We do not make food-contact claims, so use it for jars, bottles and packaged items. We need the model number from the sticker inside the fridge and photos with a ruler.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my Samsung model number?

Look on a sticker inside the fridge compartment, on a side wall or near the top. Send a photo of it with the damaged bin.

Is a printed part as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed PETG is strong in most directions, but a layer-line direction exists: the part is weakest where layers pull apart. We orient the print so the main load runs along the layers, and we tell you if a part is a poor candidate. We reinforce load-bearing corners where the original design allows.

How do I measure or photograph the part?

Photograph the bin from the front, the side and the underside, and the clips or rails that hold it on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it, from the top, the side and the mounting end. Also send the model number, usually on a sticker inside the door, under the unit or on the product label. If you still have a broken piece, photograph that too.

Is the bin food safe?

We make no food-contact claims. Keep loose food off it and use it for packaged goods and bottles.

Does the bin look identical to the original?

It will have the same shape and function. The surface shows fine print lines, and the colour is the nearest we can offer, not an exact match.
