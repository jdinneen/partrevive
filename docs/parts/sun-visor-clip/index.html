Why it breaks

The clip that holds the visor in its hook cracks or goes missing, so the visor droops or flips down on its own. It is a tiny piece of plastic on a visor that is otherwise fine. The clip is often what fails first because it flexes every time the visor is moved.

OEM situation

The clip is typically sold only as part of the visor or trim, so a single clip is hard to find. Free printable files exist for some popular models, but they need a clear photo with scale to match your car. We make one to your photo or part number instead.

What we make

We print PETG, which holds up in a hot cabin where PLA softens, and we do not use PLA for car interiors. We need a clear photo of the old clip or the empty hook, with a coin or ruler beside it, plus your year, model and trim. We make interior trim parts only, not airbag, seatbelt, brake, steering, lighting or fuel parts.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my car?

Visor clips are vehicle-specific, so we match yours from photos and your year, model and trim before printing. If it is a poor candidate for printing, we say so.

Is a printed clip as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed layers are weakest where they pull apart, so we orient the print so the flexing load runs along the layers. A visor clip is light-duty, and PETG is usually enough.

Will it soften in a hot, parked car?

PLA softens in a hot car, so we do not use it. We print PETG for cabins and can use ASA if the car sits in strong sun.

How do I photograph the clip for a fit check?

Photograph the clip from the front, side and back, and the hook it snaps into, on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it. Send your year, model, trim and any part number on the old piece.

Do you make safety-related car parts?

No. We make interior and trim parts only, and we do not make airbag, seatbelt, brake, steering, lighting or fuel parts.
