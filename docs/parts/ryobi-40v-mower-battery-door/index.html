Why it breaks

The hinged tabs on the battery bay door snap after repeated opening and years in the sun. The mower still runs, but the door will not close or stay shut.

OEM situation

An original Ryobi battery cover, part 205803001, is $34.99 at one parts seller and ships in 7 to 10 days. A used RY401017 cover sold for $27.45 on eBay in May 2025. Many covers are used or backordered, so we print one to order from $24.

What we make

We print in ASA, a plastic chosen for sun and heat, so the door can sit outside on a mower. This is a cover only. It has no battery contacts or electrical parts, and we do not make battery contacts. We need a photo of the broken door, the bay it fits and the model label on your mower. We do not claim the door is waterproof.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my Ryobi mower?

We match it from photos and your model label for Ryobi RY401014US, RY401015US and RY401017, and confirm the fit before printing. Covers are model-specific, so please check the label first.

Does it include battery contacts?

No. It is a cover only, with no battery contacts or electrical parts, and we do not make battery contacts. Your mower keeps its original contacts and wiring.

Is a printed door as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. ASA is tough, but the hinge tabs are the weak point on any printed door, so we orient the print to protect them. We tell you if your door is a poor candidate.

Will it survive sun and weather?

ASA is chosen for sun and heat, so it is a good fit for outdoor use. We do not claim the door is waterproof, so please do not rely on it to keep out heavy rain.

How do I find my model and photograph the part?

The model number is on the label on the mower, often near the deck or under the handle. Photograph the broken door, its hinge tabs and the bay beside a coin or ruler, then add the label.
