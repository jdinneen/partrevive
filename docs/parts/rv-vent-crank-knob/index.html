Why it breaks

Plastic crank knobs strip where they grip the shaft, so the handle spins without opening the vent. The knobs sit in the sun for years and turn brittle. The fit depends on the exact spline or shaft size, which varies between makers.

OEM situation

A Ventline operator with crank, BVD0462-00, was listed at $23.14 and out of stock. One etrailer answer says Ventline Northern Breeze vents are discontinued with no replacement parts. Ventline spline also differs from Jensen, so a generic knob may not fit. We price from $16.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my vent?

If we have a photo of your shaft and its measured size, we can match it. We confirm the fit with you before printing.

Is a printed knob as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed ASA is strong in most directions but weakest where the layers pull apart, so we orient the print to put the load along the layers. We tell you if a part is a poor candidate.

How do I measure the shaft?

Photograph the shaft end from the side and straight on with a coin or ruler beside it. Tell us if it is splined or round, measure the diameter across it, and measure the old knob diameter. Send the vent make or model number if you have it.

My shaft is splined. Does that matter?

Yes. Spline patterns differ between makers, and Ventline spline differs from Jensen. A clear straight-on photo of the shaft end is the most useful thing you can send.

Can I order several knobs?

Yes. Tell us how many vents you have and we quote a lower price per knob.
