Why it breaks

The small wheels under a crisper or freezer drawer flat-spot or snap, and the drawer starts to stick, scrape or tilt. The wheel is a few centimetres of plastic, but the drawer will not run without it. Owners often find the repair is not offered on its own.

OEM situation

Manufacturers typically sell only the whole rail or drawer, not the wheel. Rails are listed from about $18 to $175 depending on the brand. Printed wheels have been used by owners to get a sticking LG crisper drawer rolling again.

What we make

We print the wheel or roller in PETG, matched to your drawer from your photos, typically around 25 x 10 mm. The wheel sits inside the fridge but does not touch food. We do not claim OEM equivalence or food-contact certification. We need a photo of the broken wheel or its empty mount, the wheel diameter and axle size if you can measure them, and the model number.

Frequently asked questions

Will it fit my fridge?

Wheels vary by model, even within one brand. Send the model number and photos and we confirm the fit before printing.

Is a printed wheel as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. PETG is tough and wears reasonably, but a heavily loaded drawer will wear any plastic wheel over time. We orient the print so the wheel resists splitting along the axle and tell you if a part is a poor candidate.

Does the cold of a fridge or freezer affect it?

PETG stays usable at normal fridge and freezer temperatures in typical use. The wheel does not touch food. We do not claim food-contact certification.

How do I measure or photograph the wheel?

Measure the wheel diameter and the axle or pin size in millimetres if you can, and photograph the wheel and its mount from the side and top with a coin or ruler beside them. Also send the model number, usually on a sticker inside the fridge wall or on the door frame.

Do I need to order every wheel?

Only the ones that are broken, though flat-spotted wheels often wear together. Tell us how many you need and we quote a lower price per wheel for sets.
