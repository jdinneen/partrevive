Why it breaks

Cabinet latches flex every time the door opens, and years of heat cycles in a parked RV make the plastic brittle. When one cracks, the door swings open on the road. Some makers have discontinued the shape, so a direct replacement may not exist.

OEM situation

The Newmar 020658A latch is discontinued. A printed version of it sells for about $24 elsewhere. We price from $22 for a typical simple latch, and the final figure depends on the shape.

What we make

We print in ASA, which holds up to the heat inside a closed RV in summer. We copy the profile from your photo, your broken latch or its mounting holes. We need a clear photo with a coin or ruler, the RV make and year if you know them, and any number stamped on the part.

Frequently asked questions

My latch is discontinued. Can you still make it?

Usually, yes, if we can see the shape and the mounting points. Send photos of both sides and we tell you in the quote if anything looks unmakeable.

Is a printed part as strong as the original?

We do not claim it matches the original. Printed ASA is strong in most directions, but a layer-line direction exists: the part is weakest where layers pull apart. We orient the print so the main load runs along the layers, and we tell you if a part is a poor candidate.

How do I measure or photograph the part?

Photograph the front, back and edge of the latch, and the cutout it sits in on a plain background with a coin or ruler beside it, from the top, the side and the mounting end. Also send the model number, usually on a sticker inside the door, under the unit or on the product label. If you still have a broken piece, photograph that too.

Will it keep a cabinet door shut while driving?

It holds the door the way the original latch does if the shape matches. If the original design was marginal, a copy will be too, so a spare latch is a sensible order for heavy cabinets.

Do you make metal strikers and screws?

No. We make the plastic latch parts only.
