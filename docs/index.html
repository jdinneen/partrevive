What to expect from us

Materials we use

  • PETG for general indoor parts
  • ASA for sun and heat
  • PA-CF (carbon-fibre nylon) for high loads
  • TPU for flexible parts

What we will not make

  • Safety-critical parts such as brakes and steering
  • Battery contacts and live electrical parts
  • Children's items
  • Surfaces that touch food

Honest lead time

5 to 10 business days after approval, after you approve the quote. Quotes come back within 24 hours on business days. We are not affiliated with any brand, and we do not claim our parts match the original maker's.

Common questions

How does PartRevive work?

Send a photo of the broken part, plus the brand and model it came from. We reply with a quote within 24 hours on business days. Once you approve it we design the part, print it, and ship it. Nothing is stocked; every part is made to order.

How much does a replacement part cost?

Each part page shows a from-price for a typical quote. The final price depends on size, material and quantity, and we confirm it with you before anything is printed.

How long does it take?

You get a quote within 24 hours on business days. Making and shipping takes 5 to 10 business days after approval.

Is a 3D-printed part as strong as the original?

It depends on the material and the part. A part in a stronger material such as PA-CF can be stiffer than the original plastic, while a flexible TPU part will not be. We tell you honestly what to expect in the quote, and we do not claim equivalence with the original maker's part.

What materials do you use?

We print in PETG, ASA, PA-CF (carbon-fibre nylon), TPU. PETG suits general indoor parts, ASA suits sun and heat, PA-CF suits high loads, and TPU suits flexible parts.

What will you not make?

We do not make safety-critical parts such as brake or steering components, battery contacts or other live electrical parts, children's items, or surfaces that touch food. If you are unsure, ask and we will tell you straight.
