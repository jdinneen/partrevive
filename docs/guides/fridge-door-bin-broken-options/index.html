Cracked fridge door bin: your options and what each costs

By PartRevive · 2026-10-21

Short answer: A broken fridge door bin can be replaced with an original part, a used part, or a part printed to order. Originals are the surest fit but often cost $115 to $200 or more and are sometimes out of stock, while a printed bin typically costs less and is made to your model.

The short answer

You have three realistic options for a cracked refrigerator door bin: buy the original part, buy a used one, or have one printed to order. There is no single best choice. It depends on how much the original costs for your model, whether it is in stock, and how much fit risk you can accept.

Why door bins break

A door bin carries jars, bottles and a gallon of milk on a thin plastic shelf, and cold makes plastic more brittle. Cracks start at the corners, along the lower edge and at the small holder tabs that hook the bin to the door. Once a tab breaks, the bin sags or falls out.

The fridge itself is rarely the problem. The compressor, cooling and shelves may all be fine, so a single plastic part decides whether you keep the appliance or replace it.

Option 1: the original part

An original bin is the safest bet for fit, because it is the part the fridge was designed for. The trouble is price and supply. A Whirlpool bin plus holder can cost around $200 as a unit. Samsung door bins typically list at $115 to $140 and are often out of stock.

If you can find the right original at a fair price, buy it. Find the model number first, because fridge makers use many similar-looking bins that do not interchange.

Option 2: a used part

A used bin from a salvage seller or marketplace is cheaper, but its history is unknown. It may already have hairline cracks, and the seller may not know which models it fits. Shipping on a bulky bin can also eat the saving. Used makes sense when you can confirm the part number matches yours.

Option 3: a printed bin

We print bins to order in PETG, a tough plastic that stays strong in the cold. We make the shape from your model number and photos, rather than selling a stocked part. A printed bin is a good fit when the original is out of stock or costs more than the repair is worth.

Printed does not mean identical. The surface shows fine print lines and the color is the nearest we can offer. We make no food-contact claims, so we suggest a printed bin for jars, bottles and packaged food, not loose items. We also do not claim it is an original part or that it is certified in any way.

How to decide

  1. Find your model number, usually on a sticker inside the fridge compartment.
  2. Search for the original part by that number and note the price and stock.
  3. If the original is under about half the cost of the appliance repair you are avoiding and in stock, buy it.
  4. If it is out of stock or too costly, request a printed quote and compare.

What we need to quote a bin

Send the model number and photos of the broken bin and the door where it mounts. Put a ruler or a coin in the picture so we can read the scale, and photograph the bin from above and the side. For Whirlpool and KitchenAid style bins, see our Whirlpool and KitchenAid bin page. For Samsung, see the Samsung door bin page. If your fridge is another make, the custom part service covers it.

Strength and honesty

Printed layers are weakest where they pull apart, so we orient the print so the main load runs along them, and we tell you if your bin is a poor candidate. Heavy items such as full milk jugs are the real test, so tell us what you plan to put in it.

Frequently asked questions

Can a cracked fridge door bin be repaired instead of replaced?

Glue rarely holds, because the crack carries a load and cold plastic is brittle. A new or printed bin is usually the more reliable fix.

Where is the model number on my fridge?

Usually on a sticker inside the fresh-food compartment, on a side wall or near the top. Photograph it and send it with the broken bin.

Is a printed bin food safe?

We make no food-contact claims. Use it for jars, bottles and packaged food rather than loose food.

How long does a printed bin take?

We quote within 24 hours on business days, then print it in 5 to 10 business days after you approve the quote.
