Fixing a broken cooler latch or hinge: Yeti, RTIC, Igloo, Coleman

By PartRevive · 2026-10-21

Short answer: A cooler latch or hinge usually fails at the pin hole, where a rubber latch stretches or a plastic hinge splits. Official sets cost about $10 to $17 when they fit, and a printed TPU latch or PETG hinge makes sense when you only need one piece or the set does not match.

The short answer

Coolers outlive their small parts. The body keeps working for a decade, while a rubber pull latch tears or a plastic hinge cracks at the pin. Before you buy a whole new cooler, check whether the part is sold as a set you can afford, and whether a printed one-off would be a better fit.

Pull latches: Yeti and RTIC style

The rubber pull latch on a hard cooler wears out in two ways. The rubber tears, or the hole that sits over the pin stretches until the latch slips off. Both come from years of sun and daily pulling.

Official latch sets are sold in packs. A Yeti Tundra 2-pack was listed at about $17 at Farm and Fleet, while one reseller listed the same 2-pack at $61.39, so shop around. Larger Tundra sizes such as the 85, 120 and 155 use three latches, so a 2-pack may leave you one short.

When a printed TPU latch makes sense

TPU is a flexible plastic, so it can behave like the original rubber. We suggest it when you need a single latch rather than a pack, when one has stretched at the pin hole, or when a set is out of stock. Other small shops print latches for popular coolers too, so a printed latch is not a new idea; our advantage is matching your cooler from a photo and your measurements. We do not claim it equals the original, and we do not make any claim about resistance to animals or rough handling.

Lid hinges: Igloo and Coleman style

Plastic hinges on Igloo and Coleman coolers typically crack or split at the pin. A reviewer on MakerWorld says Igloo hinges split from stress after a few years. The good news is that stock sets are cheap:

  • Igloo Universal Hinge Set: about $10 to $12 per pair at Sportsman's Warehouse.
  • Coleman hinge set of four: about $10.43 at Walmart.
  • Hinge for a 150 to 165 quart Igloo: $35.99 from one seller.

If a universal set bolts on and the lid sits right, buy it. A printed PETG hinge is worth asking about when the universal hinge does not line up with your screw holes, when you have an older model, or when a large cooler needs a bigger hinge. Several free hinge models exist on Printables for Coleman, Igloo and Rubbermaid coolers, so we compete only on exact-fit matching from your photo.

Pelican-style case latches

Hard cases use a hook, pin and spring tab that can crack. Single latches are typically not sold on their own. ColorCase sells genuine handle-and-latch sets for the 1510, and one reviewer there wished for a single-latch option. For a printed latch we use PA-CF, a stiff carbon-fibre nylon. We make no claim about sealing or water protection; how a case seals depends on the latch preload, and we do not promise an IP rating.

What to measure

  1. Pin diameter. Measure the post or pin the latch hooks over, in millimetres. Pin-hole size is the critical number for a flexible latch.
  2. Screw spacing. Measure centre to centre between the two screw holes on a hinge or latch plate.
  3. Hinge width and thickness. Note the overall length and how thick the hinge is where it meets the pin.
  4. Number of latches. Count how many your cooler or case has, so we can quote a full set if you want one.

If you cannot measure, a photo with a coin or ruler beside the part is enough for a first quote.

What to photograph

Take the broken part on a plain surface, then the spot on the cooler where it mounts. Include the pin or the hinge pocket from the side and from above. Add any model name on the cooler if you can read it. Send everything on the request page.

Frequently asked questions

How much is an official Yeti latch set?

A Tundra 2-pack was listed at about $17 at Farm and Fleet, and one reseller listed it at $61.39. Larger Tundra sizes use three latches.

Why does a cooler hinge split?

Plastic hinges typically crack at the pin after years of stress and sun. A reviewer reports Igloo hinges splitting after a few years.

When is a printed latch better than a stock set?

When you need only one latch, the set is out of stock, or the stretched pin hole means the new part must be matched to your cooler.

What should I measure?

The pin diameter and the centre-to-centre screw spacing. A photo with a coin or ruler works if you cannot measure.

Do you make Pelican-style case latches?

Yes, in PA-CF from a photo. We do not make claims about sealing or water protection.
