RV window latch replacement: a plain guide to your options

By PartRevive · 2026-10-21

Short answer: To replace a broken RV window latch, identify the window style, then choose an original assembly, a used part, or a printed latch. Printed latches in UV-resistant ASA typically cost $7 to $24 elsewhere, and we quote from $18 per latch within 24 hours.

The short answer

An RV window latch is a small plastic part, but it is often not sold alone. You can replace it by buying the whole window assembly, finding a used latch, or having one printed in a sun-resistant plastic. For most owners, a printed latch is the quickest way to a working window without paying for an assembly.

Why RV window latches break

RV windows face direct sun, heat and cold for years. UV light makes plastic brittle, and heat cycling in a closed, parked RV speeds it up. The latch then snaps the first time you push on it, often after a decade of normal use. The window frame is usually fine.

The break often shows on windows in the style of Elixir, Hehr and Kinro, three names you will see on many travel trailer and motorhome windows. We use those names only to describe what a part fits. We are not affiliated with any of them.

Step 1: identify what you have

Start by looking at the latch and the window it came from.

  • Look for a brand or number stamped on the frame or the latch.
  • Note whether the window slides, tilts out, or hinges at the top.
  • Check how the latch mounts: screws, a clip, or a pin through the frame.
  • Photograph it from above and from the side with a coin beside it.

Why the material matters

Many printed latches are made from PLA, a common hobby plastic that softens in a hot vehicle. On a sun-facing window that is the wrong choice. We print in ASA, which is made for sun and heat, so the new latch is not the next thing to fail. See our RV window latch page for details.

Strength in plain terms

A window latch holds a window closed against wind. It is not a lock and it does not hold a person's weight. Printed ASA is strong in most directions, but a layer-line direction exists, and a latch is weakest where layers pull apart. We orient the print so the load runs along the layers.

If you also have a cabinet latch problem

The same heat that cracks window latches breaks cabinet latches. Some of those shapes are discontinued, such as the Newmar 020658A. A printed copy of that one sells for about $24 elsewhere, and we quote cabinet latches from $22. See the cabinet latch page.

Tips to make the new latch last

  • Do not force a stiff window. Clean and lubricate the track first.
  • Order a spare for each window that has the same latch, since the others are the same age.
  • Park with a sun cover where you can. UV is the main enemy of RV plastic.

Send a photo

If you are not sure what you have, send a photo anyway. We would rather tell you the part is not worth printing than quote a bad match. Use the request page to start.

What to expect when the new latch arrives

Fit the latch before you tighten any screws fully. Check that it closes the window with a firm click and that the window does not rattle. If the fit is off by a millimetre or two, tell us what you see and send a photo of the problem. We would rather adjust than leave you with a latch that nearly works.

Questions to ask any seller

  • What plastic is it made from, and is it rated for sun and heat?
  • How is the print oriented relative to the load?
  • Will they confirm the fit from your photo before taking payment?

Those three answers tell you more than a price. A cheap latch in the wrong plastic can fail in the first hot summer, and then you are doing the job twice.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my RV window latch break?

UV light and heat make the plastic brittle over the years, so it snaps under normal use. The window frame is usually still fine.

Can I buy just the latch?

Often the original is sold only as part of an assembly. Printed latches are one way to get the single part.

What plastic should an RV latch be?

ASA, because it resists UV and heat. PLA softens in a hot vehicle, so we do not use it for RV parts.

How much does a printed latch cost?

Other printed sellers charge roughly $7 to $24 per latch. We quote from $18 per latch, with lower per-unit prices for multiples.
