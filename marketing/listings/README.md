# PartRevive marketplace setup

Twelve ready-to-paste listing files sit beside this README, one per product. Prices, fit lists and materials come only from `content/products/<slug>.json`. If a fact changes, change the JSON first, then the listing. Do not add reviews, sales counts or certifications to any listing.

## Etsy shop setup: 10 steps

1. **Open the shop.** Create the Etsy shop named "PartRevive" (check the name is free; if not, ask before choosing another). Set country to United States and currency to USD.
2. **Set shop info.** Add the about text below, a banner (use `assets/img/hero.jpg`, cropped) and a logo with no brand names in it.
3. **Paste the shop policies** below into Etsy's policies section (shipping, returns, privacy).
4. **Set up the shop's "made to order" shipping profile.** Origin: your United States ZIP code. Processing time: 5 to 10 business days. Choose carrier rates or a flat rate you have checked against real parcel prices (these files do not set a shipping price).
5. **Create the listings** one at a time from the 12 files: title, 13 tags, category, price, description, then upload at least 3 real photos of your own printed parts (the captions in each file are written for this). Do not use stock or AI images of parts you have not printed.
6. **Turn on personalisation** for each listing. Mark it required, with the text "Appliance/vehicle model number and which piece broke", and note that Etsy limits the character count of this box.
7. **Mark the listing as made to order** (not "ready to ship") and set the "Who made it / What is it / When was it made" fields. Etsy treats a 3D-printed part you design and print yourself as made by you; if you use another print service, Etsy's production-partner rules apply.
8. **Check every title and description** for the line "Not affiliated with <brand>" and for "fits" or "compatible with" wording. Remove any phrase that implies OEM or genuine parts.
9. **Set up payments and tax.** Add your bank account and complete identity verification. Check your state's sales tax settings on Etsy.
10. **Publish, then test.** Place a test order or message from a second account, confirm the personalisation box and the photo-by-message flow work, and reply to the first real message within 24 hours (the site promises a quote within 24 hours on business days).

### Shop about text (2 paragraphs)

PartRevive is a one-person shop in the United States that 3D-prints replacement plastic parts to order. If a small plastic latch, handle, bin or clip has broken on an RV, a fridge, a toolbox, a cooler or a truck, and the maker no longer sells that piece on its own, send us a photo and we will tell you whether we can print one.

We do not stock parts. Every order starts with your photo or model number, and we message you a price before we print anything. We use materials suited to the job (ASA for sun and heat, PETG for everyday use, PA-CF carbon-fibre nylon for high load, TPU where the part has to flex). We are a new shop, launched October 2026, and we are not affiliated with any brand; brand names only describe what a part fits.

### Shop policies text (returns, made to order)

Made to order. Every part is printed for you after we agree the details, so we cannot accept returns or exchanges on custom parts, for example if you ordered the wrong size or changed your mind. The exception is our error. If the part does not match the agreed quote (the model, measurements or material we confirmed with you), contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos and we will replace it or refund you, your choice. If a part arrives damaged in the mail, send photos and we will make it right.

Processing time is 5 to 10 business days after approval. We ship from the United States. We confirm the final price by message before printing. Printed parts are not guaranteed to match the strength of the original. We do not make parts for food contact, battery contacts, safety systems or children's items.

## eBay checklist

1. Create or upgrade to a seller account; decide on a business account (needs your legal name and address).
2. Set a business policy for payment, shipping (from the United States) and returns. Use the returns wording above: no returns on custom parts except our error.
3. List each product using its file: title (80 characters or fewer), item specifics (Brand "Unbranded", Compatible Brand, Type, Material, Country of Manufacture United States), and the description.
4. Set handling time to the longest the eBay form allows that covers 10 business days; keep "ships in 5-10 business days" in the description. Check the form, because eBay's handling-time options may not reach 10 days for all categories.
5. Pick a fixed-price format with the "from" price, and state in the description that the final price is agreed by message before printing. Use a real photo of your own printed part.
6. Never use a brand in the Brand field or the seller name. Add "Not affiliated with <brand>" (already in each description).
7. Test: message a second account, and check that you can cancel and refund an order if you cannot make the part.
