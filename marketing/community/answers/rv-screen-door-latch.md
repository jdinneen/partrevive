Sorry that broke, that is a common one. The plastic paddle or lever on a screen door latch cracks with sun exposure and daily use, and the spring-return parts wear out. A small broken lever leaves the whole door unable to latch.

**Cheap or free fixes, in order:**

1. Full latch kits are the usual route. One zinc screen door latch kit was listed on Amazon through a third-party seller at $17.09 (price history on camelcamelcamel), so check whether a kit matches your hole spacing.
2. If the dealer has discontinued yours, as one Forest River Forums poster reported, a kit with a different handle shape may still bolt on if the hole spacing matches. Measure centre to centre before ordering.
3. Left-hand and right-hand doors need different parts, so confirm which you have.

**If none of those work:** Send a photo of the latch from inside and outside, the hole spacing in millimetres, and which hand the door is. Photos taken straight on, in good light, make matching much easier.
