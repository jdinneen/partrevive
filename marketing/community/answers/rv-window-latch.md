Sorry that broke, that is a common one. RV window latches live in direct sun and heat. Over the years the plastic turns brittle, which is why a latch that worked for a decade can snap the first time you open the window.

**Cheap or free fixes, in order:**

1. Ask the window maker or an RV parts dealer for the latch by itself. It is often sold only as part of a larger window assembly, so a small break can look like a costly repair.

3. A clean break can be held with a plastic-safe epoxy as a stopgap. Be realistic: plastic that has been in the sun for years is brittle, and the glue joint tends to fail near the old crack.
