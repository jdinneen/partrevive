## 1. Sun visor clip (universal rod clip)

**Title (max 80):** Universal Sun Visor Rod Clip, Snap-In Replacement for a Drooping Visor

**Summary:** A snap-in clip that holds a car or truck sun visor rod so the visor stops drooping. Print it in PETG or ASA so it survives a hot dashboard.

**Category:** Vehicle Parts (Printables: Hobby & Makers > Vehicle Parts)

**Description:**
The small clip that holds a sun visor rod in its hook cracks or goes missing, and the visor flops down. This is a universal rod clip you can scale to the rod on your vehicle. It fits visors that use a straight rod and a snap-in hook; it will not suit every vehicle, so measure yours first.

Parameters: rod diameter [FILL: mm], clip opening [FILL: mm], wall thickness [FILL: mm]. Measure your rod with calipers and scale the file in the slicer if the rod is not the default size. Print one, test the fit, then print a spare.

Print settings: PETG or ASA (not PLA: it softens in a hot car). Layer height 0.2 mm, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports. Orientation: lay the clip on its flat back face so the layer lines run along the length of the clip arms, not across them. Across-the-layer arms snap off.

Fitting: press the clip into the visor hook until it clicks. If it grips too loosely, scale up 1 to 2% in X/Y and reprint.

Not a safety part. It only holds a visor in place.

## 2. RV entry door holder catch

**Title (max 80):** RV Entry Door Holder Catch, Plunger and Socket Replacement, ASA

**Summary:** A replacement plunger-and-socket catch that stops a travel trailer entry door swinging in the wind. Print it in ASA for sun and weather resistance.

**Category:** Vehicle Parts

**Description:**
The plastic stem or socket on an RV entry door holder cracks or loses its grip, and the door swings open in a gust. Holders also vanish when the screws strip out. This design is a plunger-and-socket catch you can adjust to your door.

It fits typical travel trailer and caravan entry door holders of the plunger-and-socket style. Some campers use an odd or angled shape that the common store-bought catch does not fit; compare your old part before printing.

Parameters: plunger diameter [FILL: mm], socket depth [FILL: mm], screw hole spacing [FILL: mm], screw hole diameter [FILL: mm]. Measure the old holder and set the values to match.

Print settings: ASA (UV and heat resistant); PETG works for a trial fit. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports. Orientation: lay the plunger on its side so the layers run along the stem (a stem printed standing up has weak layers across it and snaps). Print the socket flat on its mounting face.

Use brass or stainless screws and do not over-tighten into the plastic.

This holds a door open in wind. It is not a lock and not safety hardware.

## 3. RV roof vent crank knob (customizable shaft)

**Title (max 80):** RV Roof Vent Crank Knob with Customizable Shaft (Spline or Round)

**Summary:** A replacement crank knob for manual RV roof vents with a shaft hole you can set to your measurements. Print it in ASA so it survives years of sun.

**Category:** Vehicle Parts

**Description:**
Plastic crank knobs strip where they grip the shaft, so the handle spins without opening the vent. They also sit in the sun for years and turn brittle. This knob has a shaft hole you can adjust, so it works with different makers' vents.

It is meant for manual roof vents of the Ventline, Elixir and Hehr style. Spline size and shaft size vary between makers, so measure your shaft before printing: do not assume it matches.

Parameters: shaft type (spline or round) [FILL: choose], shaft diameter [FILL: mm], spline tooth count [FILL: number], hole depth [FILL: mm], knob diameter [FILL: mm]. Print a quick test ring first (a thin disc with only the shaft hole) to check grip before printing the whole knob.

Print settings: ASA for sun exposure, PETG for a test. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports. Orientation: print the knob with the shaft hole pointing up so the hole is formed by walls, not bridged; the layer lines then run around the shaft, which grips better.

If the fit is loose, a drop of thread-locking compound on the shaft can help; test first.

## 4. Cooler lid hinge (universal two-leaf)

**Title (max 80):** Universal Two-Leaf Cooler Lid Hinge, Replacement for Cracked Plastic Hinges

**Summary:** A two-leaf hinge that replaces the cracked plastic hinge on common rectangular hard coolers. Print it in PETG and use a metal pin.

**Category:** Household / Outdoor (Printables: Hobby & Makers > Outdoor)

**Description:**
Cooler hinges crack and split at the pin, and the lid will not stay on even though the cooler body is fine. This is a universal two-leaf hinge that you can adjust to your cooler. A typical hinge is roughly 70 x 40 x 15 mm, and large coolers about 100 x 60 mm; confirm against your own.

It is designed for rectangular hard coolers in the style of Igloo, Coleman and Rubbermaid. Fit varies by model, so measure the screw spacing and the hinge pocket on your cooler.

Parameters: leaf length [FILL: mm], leaf width [FILL: mm], hole spacing [FILL: mm], pin diameter [FILL: mm]. Use a metal pin (a stainless rod or bolt) rather than printing the pin; a printed pin is the part that usually fails.

Print settings: PETG. 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports. Orientation: print each leaf lying flat so the layer lines run along the length of the leaf and across the pin bore, not along it. Print both leaves in the same orientation.

Seal screw holes if the cooler is used wet. Not intended for food contact surfaces.

## 5. Tonneau prop rod clip

**Title (max 80):** Tonneau Cover Prop Rod Retaining Clip, Replacement for Lost or Cracked Clips

**Summary:** A retaining clip for the prop rod or strut of a hinged truck tonneau cover. Print it in ASA so it holds up to sun and cold.

**Category:** Vehicle Parts

**Description:**
The small clip that holds a tonneau cover prop rod cracks or goes missing, so the cover will not stay propped open. That typically happens after sun, cold and many open-close cycles, while the cover itself is fine.

This clip is sized for hinged cover prop rods. Rod diameters and clip styles differ between brands, so measure your rod and compare your old clip before printing; others have posted brand-specific clips on Printables and this one is a general starting point.

Parameters: rod diameter [FILL: mm], clip opening [FILL: mm], arm thickness [FILL: mm], mounting hole [FILL: mm]. Print one and test the grip before trusting it with the cover.
