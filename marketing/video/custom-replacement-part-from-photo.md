# Video kit: custom replacement part (`custom-replacement-part-from-photo`)

Product page: https://jdinneen.github.io/partrevive/parts/custom-replacement-part-from-photo/

**Parts to print before filming:** 1 replacement of one real broken part you own, designed from photos only (this proves the photo to part flow). Pick a part that is not food-contact and not safety-critical.

**Honesty check before you post:** every part on screen was really printed by you, and every fit shown was really done. Broken parts are real, or the caption says "re-created break". No claims of OEM quality, safety, or strength versus the original. No fake reviews or order counts. If something in the video did not actually happen, cut that shot.

**Extra rule for this video:** Say "we reply with a yes, a no, or a question". Do not promise every part can be made.

Price wording: "from $35" is a typical estimate, not a list price. Lead time wording: 5 to 10 business days after approval.

## Shot list (phone only)

**Shot 1 (0:00-0:02, HOOK).** In frame: Tight close-up of the broken part in a hand, filling the frame: a real small broken plastic part from around the house (knob, clip or bracket).

**Shot 2 (0:02-0:06, THE BROKEN PART).** In frame: Phone on a tripod or a stack of books, looking straight down at the bench. The broken part on a plain surface with a US quarter or a ruler beside it for scale.

**Shot 3 (0:06-0:11, WHY IT BREAKS).** In frame: Same overhead shot, one finger pointing at the break. No new objects in frame.

**Shot 4 (0:11-0:16, THE PRINTED PART).** In frame: The finished printed part in hand, printer visible and out of focus behind it.

**Shot 5 (0:16-0:20, SIDE BY SIDE).** In frame: Overhead, printed and broken parts next to each other with a US quarter or a ruler. Slowly move the phone about 10 cm sideways to show the shape.

**Shot 6 (0:20-0:26, FIT OR INSTALL).** In frame: Over-the-shoulder shot, hands and part in frame: fit the printed copy on the thing it came from and use it once.

**Shot 7 (0:26-0:30, THE ASK).** In frame: Either face the camera, or film the product page on a laptop with the URL readable.
