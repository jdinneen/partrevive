## Shot list (phone only)

**Shot 1 (0:00-0:02, HOOK).** In frame: Tight close-up of the broken part in a hand, filling the frame: a torn rubber latch or a stretched pin hole from a real cooler (or label "re-created break").

**Shot 2 (0:02-0:06, THE BROKEN PART).** In frame: Phone on a tripod or a stack of books, looking straight down at the bench. The broken part on a plain surface with a US quarter beside it for scale.

**Shot 3 (0:06-0:11, WHY IT BREAKS).** In frame: Same overhead shot, one finger pointing at the break. No new objects in frame.

**Shot 4 (0:11-0:16, THE PRINTED PART).** In frame: The finished printed part in hand, printer visible and out of focus behind it.

**Shot 5 (0:16-0:20, SIDE BY SIDE).** In frame: Overhead, printed and broken parts next to each other with a US quarter. Slowly move the phone about 10 cm sideways to show the shape.

**Shot 6 (0:20-0:26, FIT OR INSTALL).** In frame: Over-the-shoulder shot, hands and part in frame: hook the latch over the lid lug and pull it tight, then close and open the lid twice.

**Shot 7 (0:26-0:30, THE ASK).** In frame: Either face the camera, or film the product page on a laptop with the URL readable.
